How to Reach Us

Route & Travel Guide to Gatura, Murang'a

Located just 85 km north of Nairobi, Leaf Voyage Farm is accessible via tarmac roads suitable for all standard vehicles.

Nairobi CBD to Gatura Route Breakdown

Smooth Tarmac
1

Nairobi → Thika Superhighway

Depart Nairobi CBD heading northbound along Thika Superhighway (A2) toward Thika town (40 km).

2

Thika → Thika-Gatanga-Gatura Road

Take the turnoff toward Gatanga / Kirwara / Gatura. Drive through scenic pineapple and tea green belts (38 km).

3

Gatura Shopping Centre → Leaf Voyage Farm

At Gatura town center, follow our directional signboards along the estate road (approx. 3 km to main gate).

Driving Time 1 hr 45 min
Vehicle Type All Cars / Sedans OK
Public Transport Matatu from Thika Stage
