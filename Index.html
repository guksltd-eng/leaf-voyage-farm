Become an Ambassador Visitor Assistance Frequently Asked Questions Everything you need to know before planning your visit to Gatura. What is included in the full Tea Farm Tour package? The full day tour package includes a guided walk through the green tea rows, hands-on tea plucking instruction, a live cottage factory processing demonstration, master tea tasting of 6 specialty teas, a 3-course farm-to-table lunch, and a guided hike down to the private estate waterfall. Do I need to book in advance? Yes, advance reservations are required to ensure fresh ingredient preparation for fine dining and to maintain small, intimate tour group sizes. We recommend booking at least 24 to 48 hours in advance for weekends. Is the tour suitable for children and seniors? Yes! The tea walk trails are gentle and accessible. We tailor walking speeds to suit all age groups, and comfortable seating areas are located throughout the processing unit and dining garden. What should I wear for the farm visit? We recommend comfortable walking shoes or sneakers, a sun hat or cap, and a light jacket as mountain breezes near Aberdare can be cool in the early morning or late afternoon.
